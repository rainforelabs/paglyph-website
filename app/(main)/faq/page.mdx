# Frequently asked questions

<details className={styles.item}>
  <summary>What is Paglyph?</summary>

Paglyph is a reading companion app that helps you understand, save, and review the words you find while reading. Take a photo of a page, tap a word, explore its meaning in context, and build a vocabulary library.

</details>

<details className={styles.item}>
  <summary>How do I capture a word?</summary>

Point your camera at a page and tap a word. Paglyph brings its sentence along automatically, so you can explore the meaning without typing it out.

</details>

<details className={styles.item}>
  <summary>How is Paglyph different from a dictionary?</summary>

A word can mean different things in different sentences. Paglyph combines dictionary definitions with AI-powered explanations to help you understand how a word is being used in your reading.

</details>

<details className={styles.item}>
  <summary>Can I save words for later?</summary>

Yes. Save words with their definitions and original sentences in your personal library. When you return to a word, the context you discovered it in is there too.

</details>

<details className={styles.item}>
  <summary>How does Paglyph help me remember new words?</summary>

Review saved words with flashcards and rate how well you remember them. Paglyph uses your responses to schedule future reviews. You can also set a daily word goal and a review reminder.

</details>

<details className={styles.item}>
  <summary>Does Paglyph work offline?</summary>

Your saved library is available offline while you’re signed in and have access to the app. New lookups and AI explanations require an internet connection.

</details>

<details className={styles.item}>
  <summary>Can I sync my library?</summary>

Yes. With iCloud sync enabled, your saved words, review progress, and learning preferences can sync across devices using the same iCloud account. You can manage this in Settings.

</details>

<details className={styles.item}>
  <summary>Do I need an account?</summary>

Yes. Use your Apple Account to create or access your Paglyph account.

</details>

<details className={styles.item}>
  <summary>Does Paglyph require a subscription?</summary>

Yes. Paglyph offers monthly and yearly subscriptions. You’ll see the current price and any eligible free trial in the app before subscribing.

</details>

<details className={styles.item}>
  <summary>Where can I manage my subscription?</summary>

Open Settings in Paglyph, then tap your profile at the top to manage your subscription.

</details>
